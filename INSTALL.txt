VividHUD — Installation Guide
=============================

VividHUD shows a big, clear volume and brightness pop-up in the middle of your
screen whenever you use the volume or brightness keys. It runs quietly in the
background and starts automatically each time you turn on your Mac.


INSTALL (about 20 seconds)
--------------------------
1. Double-click VividHUD.dmg to open it (you may have already done this).
2. Drag the VividHUD icon onto the Applications folder shown next to it.
3. That's it — you can now close and eject the disk image.


FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT  (important!)
------------------------------------
Because this is a free app that isn't registered with Apple, macOS shows a
one-time warning the first time you open it. This is normal. To get past it:

   1. Open your Applications folder.
   2. RIGHT-CLICK (or Control-click) VividHUD.
   3. Choose "Open".
   4. In the box that appears, click "Open" again.

You only have to do this ONCE. After that it opens normally.

If you don't see an "Open" button, go to:
   Apple menu  >  System Settings  >  Privacy & Security
Scroll down — you'll see a message about VividHUD and an "Open Anyway" button.
Click it, then click "Open".


HOW TO USE IT
-------------
- Just press your volume or brightness keys — the pop-up appears automatically.
- Look in the top-right of your menu bar for a small slider icon. Click it for:
      • Start at Login   — turn automatic startup on/off (on by default)
      • Test HUD         — show the pop-up so you know it's working
      • Quit VividHUD    — stop the app


DOES IT START AUTOMATICALLY AFTER A RESTART?
--------------------------------------------
Yes. The first time you open it, it adds itself to your login items, so it will
be running every time you start your Mac. You can confirm or change this under:
   System Settings  >  General  >  Login Items
   (or use the "Start at Login" checkbox in the menu-bar icon).


UNINSTALL
---------
1. Click the slider icon in the menu bar and uncheck "Start at Login".
2. Click the slider icon again and choose "Quit VividHUD".
3. Open Applications and drag VividHUD to the Trash.

The login-item entry clears itself once the app is removed. If you ever want to
tidy it manually, it's under System Settings > General > Login Items.


PERMISSIONS
-----------
VividHUD does not need any special permissions (no Accessibility access needed).


REQUIREMENTS
------------
macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer.
